What is actually checked?

L1 proof grounding and closure, generated L3 tables, candidate universes, integer bounds, provenance, and every semantic query.

The checker is an independent JavaScript implementation, not formally verified or extracted from Lean. The seed assumptions and synonym fibers are unreviewed. Raw-English interpretation and dialogue policy have separate traces. L5 discovery and multi-agent allocation are outside this app’s scope.
