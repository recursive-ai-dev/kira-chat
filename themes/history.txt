# History: periods, people and the stories we keep retelling. Cue-only: Kira does not assert facts.
topic: history
open: Is it a period, a person, or an event that pulls you in? | What would you most like to see for yourself, if you could stand there for a day?
ask: What do you think people living then understood least about their own time? | Is there a story from it that you find yourself retelling? | What does it explain about the world you live in now? | Where did you first come across it? | What do you wish more people knew about it?
