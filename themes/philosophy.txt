topic: philosophy
open: Philosophy usually starts with a question that will not stay quiet. What is yours? | Is that something you have turned over for a long time, or did it just surface?
ask: What question keeps coming back to you? | Where do you land on it today, and has that moved over time? | Is there a thinker or a book that shaped how you see it? | What would actually change your mind? | How does it show up in your ordinary days?
